# Dealer Profitability Simulator – Indian Automotive Retail

A business intelligence model designed to simulate real dealer profitability in the Indian automotive industry.

This project focuses on a common but under-analysed problem:

> A dealership can achieve high retail volume but still operate at a financial loss.

The simulator helps visualize the true unit economics behind a vehicle sale by combining:

- OEM margin
- Dealer discounting
- Finance penetration
- Insurance attachment
- Exchange bonus
- Incentives
- Service retention impact

---

## Why this project exists

In the Indian automotive ecosystem, performance is often measured only on:

Retail numbers  
Billing achievement  
Market share  

However, dealer sustainability depends on **per-vehicle profitability**, not volume alone.

This tool enables:
- OEM sales teams to evaluate campaign impact
- Dealer principals to understand margin leakages
- Network planners to assess viability
- Strategy teams to simulate policy changes

---

## What the simulator calculates

**Net Dealer Contribution Per Vehicle**

Revenue Streams:
- Vehicle margin
- Finance commission
- Insurance commission
- Exchange commission
- OEM incentives
- Accessories

Cost Leakages:
- Consumer discount
- Subvention burden
- Overheads allocation
- Inventory interest cost

Future Module:
Service lifetime value (LTV)
